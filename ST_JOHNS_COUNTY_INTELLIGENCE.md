## 7. Key Findings

### Most Useful Real-Time Intelligence Sources
1. **SJSO News Stories** (https://www.sjso.org/news-stories/) -- Best source for real-time incident reporting, arrests, public safety alerts. Last updated April 2026.
2. **SJC News** (https://www.sjcfl.us/news/) -- County government press releases on policy, development, and services.
3. **SJSO Social Media** (@SJSOPIO on X/Twitter) -- Rapid public safety notifications and incident awareness.
4. **Development Tracker** (https://www.sjcfl.us/development-tracker/) -- Real-time map of development projects across the county.

### Gaps
- **RSS/API Feeds:** Neither main site appears to offer structured feeds (RSS/Atom) for automated monitoring. Would require scraping or HTML parsing.
- **Agendas/Minutes:** URL has moved post-redesign. Location needs discovery through site navigation or search.
- **County Calendar:** Listed on homepage but not independently scraped -- likely embedded or plugin-driven.
- **BoardDocs:** School Board uses BoardDocs platform for document management; this is a separate system with its own authentication.

### Tech Stack Notes
- SJC County: WordPress (New Relic monitoring, GTM/GA4)
- SJSO: WordPress (responsive theme, no obvious analytics visible)
- School District: WordPress (Astra theme, Elementor page builder)
- Nocatee: HubSpot CMS
