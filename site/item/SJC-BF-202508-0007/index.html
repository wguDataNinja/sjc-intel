SJCSD Releases Revised Attendance Zoning Proposal Following Community Feedback

Source published
August 15, 2025

Summary

St. Johns County School District released a revised attendance zoning proposal after receiving community input on initial boundary recommendations. The revised proposal addresses concerns from affected neighborhoods and adjusts proposed zone lines for schools serving the Silverleaf and Nocatee growth corridors.

Why it matters

Revised boundaries directly impact school assignments and may affect property values and daily commutes for families in affected neighborhoods.
