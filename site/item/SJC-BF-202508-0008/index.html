SJCSD Presents Final Attendance Zoning Plans for 2025-2026 School Year

Source published
August 20, 2025

Summary

St. Johns County School District presented final attendance zoning plans establishing school attendance boundaries for the 2025-2026 school year. The plans incorporate adjustments for the opening of Hallowes Cove Academy and address capacity needs across the district's growing areas including Silverleaf and Nocatee.

Why it matters

Final attendance zones determine school eligibility for students and affect planning for families with school-aged children, particularly in areas experiencing rezoning due to new school openings.
