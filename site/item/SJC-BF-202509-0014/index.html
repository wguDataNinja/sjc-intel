Raised Pavement Markers Replacement Program

Source published
September 29, 2025

Summary

St. Johns County continued its Raised Pavement Markers Replacement Program, installing new reflective markers on county roads to improve nighttime visibility and driving safety.

Why it matters

Well-maintained pavement markers are critical for safe nighttime driving, especially on rural and unlit county roads.
