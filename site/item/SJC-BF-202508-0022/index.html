Inside SJC Episode 5: Notifying Residents Using Alert St. Johns Emergency Notification System

Source published
August 5, 2025

Summary

St. Johns County launched Inside SJC Episode 5, highlighting how the county notifies residents in real-time about possible threats to public safety using Alert St. Johns — covering severe weather, unexpected road closures, water advisories, missing persons, or evacuations. The episode educates residents on how to sign up for emergency alerts.

Why it matters

The emergency notification system is a critical tool for public safety, enabling residents to receive real-time alerts about threats and take appropriate protective actions.
