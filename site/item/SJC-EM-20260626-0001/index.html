Source published

Summary

St. Johns County Emergency Management urges residents to prepare for the 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season, which began June 1. Highlights simple steps to protect lives and property.

Why it matters

June 1 marks the start of hurricane season. Residents should review plans, sign up for Alert St. Johns, and know their evacuation zone.
