Baptist Health SilverLeaf — Orthopedics opening August 2026, Emergency Center opening September 2026

Source published
August 9, 2026

Summary

Baptist Health's SilverLeaf Medical Campus (8595 St. Johns Parkway) is expanding: Baptist Orthopedics opens August 2026 and the adult/pediatric Emergency Center opens September 2026, adding 24/7 emergency care to the campus's existing primary care, cardiology, lab, and rehab services.

Why it matters

An emergency center inside SilverLeaf shortens critical care access; orthopedics adds a common specialty without driving to Jacksonville.
