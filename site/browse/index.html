First Coast Expressway — St. Johns County final phase and I-95 access

FDOT's First Coast Expressway (SR 23) is being built in segments; the remaining St. Johns County work reached the public-hearing stage in January 2026 and FDOT targets full completion by 2030. SilverLeaf's commute options and access to I-95 will change as the expressway completes; residents should expect detours on St. Johns Parkway during final-phase construction.
