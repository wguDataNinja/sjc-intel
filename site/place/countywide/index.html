SJRWMD Phase III Extreme Water Shortage — One-Day-Per-Week Irrigation

SJRWMD Phase III Extreme Water Shortage effective May 11. Residential watering limited to one day per week between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Reclaimed water exempt. Extreme Drought (D3) conditions cited.

Why it matters

Mandatory irrigation limits affect all properties with landscaping. Reclaimed water users exempt. Penalties for non-compliance.
