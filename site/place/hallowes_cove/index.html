Hallowes Cove Academy Welcomes First Students for Inaugural School Year

Hallowes Cove Academy, St. Johns County School District's newest K-8 school, welcomed its first cohort of students for the 2025-2026 school year in September. Led by Principal Jessley Hathaway with the Hurricanes mascot, the school serves grades K-8 with extended day programs in the Silverleaf area.

Why it matters

The school's opening reduces overcrowding at nearby schools and provides a modern educational facility for families in one of Florida's fastest-growing counties.

Hallowes Cove Academy Opens as St. Johns County's Newest K-8 School for 2025-2026

Hallowes Cove Academy, St. Johns County School District's newest K-8 school, opened its doors for the 2025-2026 school year. The school, led by Principal Jessley Hathaway and featuring the Hurricanes mascot, serves students in grades K-8 with extended day programs. The school's dedicated website and branding materials were finalized in April 2025 ahead of the August opening.

Why it matters

This new school relieves enrollment pressure on existing schools in the northwest St. Johns County corridor and offers a new educational option for families in the rapidly developing Silverleaf area.
