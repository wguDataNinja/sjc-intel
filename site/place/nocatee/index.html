Exclusive: Here's what coming to new Nocatee retail center across from water park

An Ascension St. Vincent's primary care center is planned for a six-storefront commercial strip along Crosswater Parkway in Nocatee, directly across from Splash Water Park. The center will occupy three of the six units, providing adult and pediatric care including physicals, screenings, and treatment for minor illnesses and injuries.

Why it matters

Brings accessible healthcare directly into Nocatee's commercial core, making routine checkups and minor medical care more convenient for residents.

SJCSD Begins Community Engagement for Naming and Mascot Selection of Schools QQ and RR

St. Johns County School District initiated community engagement for the naming and mascot selection of two new K-8 schools: School QQ in Silverleaf and School RR in Nocatee. Virtual meetings were held to gather input from residents on school names and mascots for the schools projected to open for the 2026-2027 school year.

Why it matters

School names and mascots establish community identity and school culture for decades to come. Community input ensures the schools reflect local heritage and values.

SJCSD Provides Construction Update on K-8 Schools QQ and RR in Silverleaf and Nocatee

St. Johns County School District provided an update on two new K-8 schools under construction: School QQ in the Silverleaf DRI (Parcel 29C) and School RR in Nocatee's Seabrook area (formerly Snowden Village). Both schools have a capacity of approximately 1,500 students each and are projected to open for the 2026-2027 school year.

Why it matters

These schools will relieve enrollment pressure on existing schools in the northwest and northeast growth corridors when they open for the 2026-2027 school year.

County Encourages Alternate Access Points to Beachwalk During CR 210 Widening

St. Johns County encouraged drivers accessing the Beachwalk community to consider alternate routes during the ongoing County Road 210 widening project. Motorists were reminded to use caution and follow posted signage.

Why it matters

The CR 210 widening project is one of the largest road infrastructure projects in the county, affecting daily commutes for thousands of residents in northern St. Johns County.
