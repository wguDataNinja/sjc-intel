County Road 16A Weekend Closure Scheduled August 7–10

County Road 16A is scheduled to close between Timberwolf Trail and River Reach Parkway from 8 p.m. Friday, August 7, through 5 a.m. Monday, August 10, for First Coast Expressway pipe installation. Drivers will be detoured via State Roads 16 and 13.

Why it matters

The closure may affect travel between SilverLeaf and destinations east of the community during the weekend.
