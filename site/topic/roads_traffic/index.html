First Coast Expressway final segment under construction — $205M contract, completion late 2030/early 2031

FDOT's final St. Johns County segment of the First Coast Expressway — from I-95 to east of CR 2209 (St. Johns Parkway) — is under construction with a $205 million contract awarded to Mastec Civil LLC. FDOT lists construction start in early 2026 and expected completion in late 2030/early 2031. A hybrid construction open house was held Feb. 3, 2026 at World Golf Village.

Why it matters

The toll expressway changes SilverLeaf commute options; I-95/CR 2209/CR 16A lane-closure restrictions during construction affect the surrounding road network.

First Coast Expressway — St. Johns County final phase and I-95 access

FDOT's First Coast Expressway (SR 23) is being built in segments; the remaining St. Johns County work reached the public-hearing stage in January 2026 and FDOT targets full completion by 2030. When complete, the toll expressway gives northwestern St. Johns County, including SilverLeaf, a new north-south alternative to I-95, and construction has required temporary St. Johns Parkway detours. Status as of early August 2026: final-phase work underway.

Why it matters

SilverLeaf's commute options and access to I-95 will change as the expressway completes; residents should expect detours on St. Johns Parkway during final-phase construction.

SR 16 RCUT intersection improvements — new traffic patterns at Stratton Boulevard and Industry Center Road expected mid-to-late September

St. Johns County Public Works is finishing FDOT-recommended Restricted Crossing U-Turn (RCUT) intersection improvements on SR 16 at Stratton Boulevard and Industry Center Road. Signalized U-turns replace unsignalized left turns, with additional turn lanes, new signals, and two new pedestrian crosswalks; the new traffic pattern is expected to start mid-to-late September.

Why it matters

A changed traffic pattern on a commute corridor is a practical 'what to expect' for drivers using SR 16 near the World Golf Village/outlet area.

County Road 2209 Roadway Expansion Underway in St. Johns County

St. Johns County commenced construction on a major roadway expansion project along County Road 2209, building a new four-lane corridor between Silverleaf Parkway and State Road 16 to improve traffic flow in the northern part of the county. The project supports long-term mobility needs and upholds the county's obligations to FDOT to alleviate traffic challenges on I-95.

Why it matters

This is a major corridor project that will transform transportation in the fastest-growing area of the county, connecting two major east-west routes (CR 210 and SR 16) and relieving I-95 congestion.
