Baptist SilverLeaf outpatient medical campus now open

Baptist Health opened its first-phase outpatient medical campus in SilverLeaf on June 23, 2026, adding hospital-grade outpatient care (imaging, labs, physician offices and an emergency center component) to the community. The campus was permitted for $16.8 million in January 2025 and received approval for a helipad addition in April 2026. Baptist Health is the facility operator and a first-party source for its own campus.

Why it matters

Closer access to Baptist outpatient services and emergency-adjacent care is a significant household convenience; a helipad approval signals the campus can support air-transport when needed.

Fore! Major new 2-acre recreational attraction proposed for SilverLeaf by husband and wife team

Beach Valley Mini Golf, a 36-hole mini-golf course with a clubhouse, pavilions, and parking, has been proposed on 2 acres in SilverLeaf.
