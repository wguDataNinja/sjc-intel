Silverleaf Market — Publix-anchored center adds outparcels and tenants

The Publix-anchored shopping center known as Silverleaf Market is adding outparcel buildings, with reporting that planning for additional structures advanced in August 2026. Nearby retail activity at the center includes plans for a Fifth Third Bank (reported October 2025 and advancing April 2026) and new food tenants M Shack and Natural Greens (reported July 2026). Here's your first look at SilverLeaf's new 55,000 sq. ft. mega Publix

A massive new Publix — one of the chain's largest at 55,701 sq. ft. — has opened at 1975 SilverLeaf Parkway, giving SilverLeaf residents a full-service grocery with specialty departments including a Pours café serving beer and wine, a scratch bakery, custom-cut meats, fresh seafood, a full-service pharmacy, and prepared food stations.

Why it matters

Adds daily grocery shopping convenience within walking/driving distance of SilverLeaf homes, with specialty food and beverage options that function as a local gathering spot.
