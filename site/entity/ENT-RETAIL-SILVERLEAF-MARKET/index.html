Silverleaf Market — Publix-anchored center adds outparcels and tenants

The Publix-anchored shopping center known as Silverleaf Market is adding outparcel buildings, with reporting that planning for additional structures advanced in August 2026. Nearby retail activity at the center includes plans for a Fifth Third Bank (reported October 2025 and advancing April 2026) and new food tenants M Shack and Natural Greens (reported July 2026). Tenant-by-tenant timing is not yet confirmed.

Why it matters

New bank and restaurant tenants plus outparcel construction are visible neighborhood change; the center's growth is directly useful household knowledge.
