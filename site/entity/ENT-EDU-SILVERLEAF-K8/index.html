In SilverLeaf Schools & Community Latest Reviewed Opening for 2026-27

Magnolia Oaks Academy set to open for the 2026-2027 school year

St. Johns County School District lists Magnolia Oaks Academy as the new K-8 school serving the SilverLeaf DRI (Parcel 29C), which the district confirms remains under construction. Local reporting (News4JAX, Action News Jax) says the campus is set to open for the 2026-2027 school year with about 1,500 students across 73 classrooms. The school was previously tracked under the placeholder names School QQ and SilverLeaf K-8.

Why it matters

The opening may change attendance zones and shorten school commutes for SilverLeaf households; the final opening date and attendance boundary are the top school questions.

In SilverLeaf Schools & Community Latest Reviewed Opening Aug 10

Magnolia Oaks Academy opens Aug. 10 with 1,300 students — K-8 school in Silverleaf

SJCSD confirms Magnolia Oaks Academy will welcome 1,300 students on its first day, Aug. 10, 2026, serving kindergarten through seventh grade in its first year. The district opened the doors announcement (July 22, 2026) confirms the school is the first in the Silverleaf community. The 2026-27 attendance plan for K-8 School QQ (Magnolia Oaks) was approved by the School Board Nov. 18, 2025 and the district's attendance-zone locator is live.

Why it matters

Aug. 10 first day, K-7 grade span, and the live attendance-zone locator answer the top school questions for SilverLeaf households for 2026-27.
