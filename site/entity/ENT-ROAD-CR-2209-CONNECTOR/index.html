Three-mile, four-lane divided roadway connecting International Golf Parkway to SilverLeaf Parkway. Opened October 28, 2025. Relieves congestion around Tocoi Creek High School (2,700+ students) and provides a direct SilverLeaf-to-IGP route.

In SilverLeaf Roads & Traffic Timeline Reviewed Completed

Road relief: Here's your first look at St. Johns County's newest highway

County Road 2209 opened October 28, 2025, connecting International Golf Parkway to SilverLeaf Parkway with a four-lane divided roadway spanning three miles. The new route aims to relieve congestion in the rapidly growing area, particularly around Tocoi Creek High School which serves over 2,700 students.

Why it matters

Directly improves daily commute times for SilverLeaf residents heading to I-95, World Golf Village, and western St. Johns County employment centers.
