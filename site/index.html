County Road 16A Closures and Detours for Next Two Weekends

Weekend closures of County Road 16A between Timberwolf Trail and River Reach Parkway are scheduled 8 p.m. Friday to 5 a.m. Monday (Jul 31-Aug 3 and Aug 7-10) for First Coast Expressway pipe installation. Detour via SR 16 and SR 13.

Why it matters

County Road 16A closures and detours over consecutive weekends affect local travel.

Utility Department Water Service Line Material Inventory

The county utility is inventorying water service line materials under the EPA Community Lead-Safe Hub program. Customers may be contacted to allow an inspection of the pipe from the street to the home.

Why it matters

Lead pipe identification program affects drinking water safety. Some residents may be contacted for property access to inspect service lines. No lead pipes confirmed; this is a proactive inventory.

Residents Urged to Prepare as 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season Begins

St. Johns County Emergency Management urges residents to prepare for the 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season, which began June 1. Highlights simple steps to protect lives and property.

Why it matters

June 1 marks the start of hurricane season. Residents should review plans, sign up for Alert St. Johns, and know their evacuation zone.

Phase III Extreme Water Shortage Declaration — Active

The Phase III Extreme Water Shortage remains in effect: outdoor irrigation is limited to one day per week and is prohibited 8 a.m.-6 p.m. Reclaimed-water irrigation is not included in the schedule.

Why it matters

Ongoing irrigation restrictions affect all properties with landscaping. Reclaimed water users exempt. Violations may result in penalties. Drought conditions may lead to higher water costs.
