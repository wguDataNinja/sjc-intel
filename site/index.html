In SilverLeaf Schools & Community Latest Reviewed Opening for 2026-27

Magnolia Oaks Academy set to open for the 2026-2027 school year

St. Johns County School District lists Magnolia Oaks Academy as the new K-8 school serving the SilverLeaf DRI (Parcel 29C), which the district confirms remains under construction. Local reporting (News4JAX, Action News Jax) says the campus is set to open for the 2026-2027 school year with about 1,500 students across 73 classrooms. The school was previously tracked under the placeholder names School QQ and SilverLeaf K-8.

Why it matters

The opening may change attendance zones and shorten school commutes for SilverLeaf households; the final opening date and attendance boundary are the top school questions.

CR 210 widening completed in time for the 2026-2027 school year

The county's multi-year CR 210 widening project reached a major completion milestone ahead of the 2026-2027 school year, with local reporting (News4JAX) indicating the new roadway opened before the first day of school. The project had shifted traffic onto newly built lanes in August 2025 as part of the widening through northern St. Johns County.

Why it matters

CR 210 is one of the county's busiest east-west corridors; the completed widening relieves congestion for SilverLeaf commuters.

Countywide impact Utilities & Water Latest Reviewed

Phase III Extreme Water Shortage Declaration — Active

The Phase III Extreme Water Shortage remains in effect: outdoor irrigation is limited to one day per week and is prohibited 8 a.m.-6 p.m. Reclaimed-water irrigation is not included in the schedule.

Why it matters

SilverLeaf households using potable water for irrigation must follow the one-day-per-week schedule and avoid watering between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Properties using reclaimed water are exempt from the one-day schedule.
