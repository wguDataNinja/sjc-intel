County Road 16A Weekend Closure Scheduled August 7–10

County Road 16A is scheduled to close between Timberwolf Trail and River Reach Parkway from 8 p.m. Friday, August 7, through 5 a.m. Monday, August 10, for First Coast Expressway pipe installation. Drivers will be detoured via State Roads 16 and 13.

Why it matters

The closure may affect travel between SilverLeaf and destinations east of the community during the weekend.

Utility Department Water Service Line Material Inventory

The county utility is inventorying water service line materials under the EPA Community Lead-Safe Hub program. Customers may be contacted to allow an inspection of the pipe from the street to the home.

Why it matters

The inventory is intended to identify service-line materials and determine whether any further action is needed. Some county utility customers may be contacted to allow an inspection between the street and the home.

Countywide impact Utilities & Water Reviewed

Phase III Extreme Water Shortage Declaration — Active

The Phase III Extreme Water Shortage remains in effect: outdoor irrigation is limited to one day per week and is prohibited 8 a.m.-6 p.m. Reclaimed-water irrigation is not included in the schedule.

Why it matters

SilverLeaf households using potable water for irrigation must follow the one-day-per-week schedule and avoid watering between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Properties using reclaimed water are exempt from the one-day schedule.
