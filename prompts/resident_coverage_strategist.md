# Resident Coverage Strategist task contract

Inputs are a visible-state cutoff, dated public evidence, prior accepted
proposals, source/search health, and unresolved gaps. Do not inspect an
evaluator baseline, future evidence, production review decisions, or future
aliases. Produce evidence-backed machine-readable proposals only. A separate
evaluator decides `accepted`, `rejected`, `revision_required`, `blocked`, or
`unsafe_to_continue`; accepted changes are visible in the next week.
