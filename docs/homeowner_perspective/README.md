# SJC_Intel — Homeowner Perspective

Cross-cutting summaries of what matters to St. Johns County residents,
reading across all available data. Updated after each backfill or monitor cycle.

## Current Themes from Available Data

### May 2026 Dominant Story: Water Crisis

The Phase III Extreme Water Shortage was the single highest-impact event.
One-day-per-week irrigation, Extreme Drought (D3), reclaimed water exemption.
Affects every SJC resident with landscaping. `water_restrictions` taxonomy
gap promoted to canonical based on this evidence.

### School District Activity

10 items from SJCSD in May — programs, funding, policy, graduations.
High signal-to-noise ratio (~40% actionable). St. Johns Compass program and
Programs of Choice are the most resident-relevant items.

### Public Safety

Two SJSO stories: contraband incident (negative, requires human review)
and lifesaving rescue (positive, community programs). Normal cadence for
a sheriff's office.
